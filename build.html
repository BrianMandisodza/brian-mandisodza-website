How I Approach Building

Ideas are cheap.
Execution isn't.

I approach opportunities by testing the underlying assumptions before committing resources. The objective is not to make every idea work. It is to identify which ideas deserve to be pursued.

01

Start with the problem.

A business becomes interesting when it solves a real problem for someone willing to pay for the solution.

02

Test the economics.

Revenue alone doesn't make a business attractive. The underlying economics need to make sense.

03

Understand the downside.

Before asking how much can be made, understand what can go wrong and how much can be lost.

04

Find the right partners.

Some problems are solved faster through collaboration than through trying to build everything alone.
